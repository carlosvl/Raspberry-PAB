# MCA 2026 season

middle school. For how the standings are computed, see

- **Roseville schedule (confirmed by user, 2026-09-27):** Xcel, Lake Rebecca,

  Roseville **HS ÷ 3** (Xcel, Rebecca, Cuyuna), because the canceled Theo HS
  day is left out of the points and the count.
- **Example, Carlos Mateo Villalpando (JV3 Boys):** 314 + 302 = 616 ÷ 2 =
  308.0, #130/196; the #100 average is 330.0. Final = (616 + X) ÷ 3, so he
  needs X ≈ 376 (≈28th) at Cuyuna. Without the rainout rule the canceled race
  would have been a 0: 616 ÷ 3 = 205.3, about #178.
