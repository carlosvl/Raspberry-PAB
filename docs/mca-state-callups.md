## How call-ups are ordered

The standard rule (Sporting Regulations Ch. 9) applies: riders are called up by
their **average finishing place** across this season's regular-season races
that they finished. Missed races and byes are left out. The lowest average goes
first.

- **Who's on the list:** only State qualifiers. That means the top 100 in the
  category (or the whole field when it has 100 or fewer riders), with at least 2
  races.
- **Freshman Girls and JV2 Girls** start together, so they share one call-up
  order, as they did at Cuyuna.
- **Checked against MCA:** this method reproduces MCA's own Cuyuna call-up
  numbers exactly (Nora Walz 1.6667, Ilene Shaffner 16.0, Theodor Boulton 18.5,
  Charlie Dixon 5.6667, Logan Vargas 21.0).
- **Waves:** wave sizes for State aren't published yet, so only the position is
  shown. At Cuyuna, waves held about 30–50 riders.
