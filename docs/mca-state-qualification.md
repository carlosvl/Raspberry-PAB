## How canceled races count

Season average = points from the regular-season races the team was **scheduled for and that were held** ÷ the number of those races (Ch. 11 p. 19). A missed race counts as 0 and stays in the average. A canceled race is **left out of both the points and the count** (/3 instead of /4), but it still uses one of the team's 4 race slots, so it isn't replaced by another race.

- **Roseville MS:** held so far 2, 3A, 5B, Cuyuna 10/3. With 0 race(s) left, the final average divides by **4**.
  - Example, Nora Walz: now 1970 ÷ 4 = 492.5; after the last race (1970 + X) ÷ 4, so that race is worth 1/4 of the season.
- **Roseville HS:** held so far 2, 3A, Cuyuna 10/3. With 0 race(s) left, the final average divides by **3**.
  - Example, Ryan Kokotovich: now 1454 ÷ 3 = 484.7; after the last race (1454 + X) ÷ 3, so that race is worth 1/3 of the season.
- Riders on teams that were at the canceled event lose nothing and gain nothing. Their average simply has one fewer race, so each remaining race weighs more.
